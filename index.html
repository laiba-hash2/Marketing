Best Practices & Strategy

Proven frameworks used by top advertisers — explained simply

🎯

Define Your Audience

Before spending a single dollar, know exactly who you're talking to. Age, interests, location, income level — the more specific, the better your ROI.

Pro Tip: Start with a "lookalike" audience based on your existing customers.
✍️

Write Compelling Copy

Your ad text should speak directly to a pain point or desire. Use the formula: Problem → Solution → CTA. Keep it short and punchy.

Pro Tip: Lead with a question your customer is already asking themselves.
🖼️

Use Strong Visuals

People scroll fast. Your image or video has less than 2 seconds to stop them. Use bold colors, faces, and minimal text on images.

Pro Tip: Video ads consistently outperform static images by 20–30%.
🧪

Always A/B Test

Never run just one ad. Test 2–3 variations of your headline, image, or audience. Let the data tell you what works — then scale it.

Pro Tip: Change only ONE variable per test so you know what caused the difference.
🔄

Retargeting

Most people don't buy on the first visit. Retargeting shows ads to people who've already visited your site — these convert 10x better.

Pro Tip: Install your pixel/tag on your website before you start spending money on ads.
📊

Track & Optimize

Check your campaigns every 3–7 days. Kill what's not working. Double down on winners. Small tweaks compound into big results over time.

Pro Tip: Focus on Cost Per Result (CPR), not just clicks or impressions.
